RULES - Academia

  1. If coming home from school, get refreshed and use phone for 10-20 minutes maximum to get caught up. Have lunch at the same time.
  2. Do not eat lunch and watch phone separately. If you have to pick one, pick lunch and finish it.
  3. Immediately finish all homework for the day.
  4. If going for a nap, set an alarm for 1 hour, or 1.5 hours maximum.
  5. Wake up, get refreshed. Regardless of what time it is, begin your first 2 hour long study session.
  6. Take a break of maximum 10-15 minutes.
  7. Start next study session of 2 hours.
  8. Have a break after this infinitely until dinner time, or revise what you did in the meantime until the same.
  9. After dinner, begin a 1 hour study session, followed by a 1 hour revision time of everything studied in the day.
  10. The last 1 hour revision can be done while walking, listening to music etc, as a lighthearted activity so long as you are able to ensure that the revision is actually happening since it is crucial.

RULES - Holistic

  1. In the free hours left, use the internet to find 1 random topic of your own interest. Once a topic is found, that topic's week has begun.
  2. Open a notes app on your phone, iPad, or even pen and paper, and write down all cool facts and information about that topic.
  3. Do not rush, this is your topic for a whole week, so there is plenty of research time.
  4. At the end of the week you have to submit as a PDF your 700-1000 word essay, written in your own language.
  5. Usage of AI is strictly forbidden because it is a matter of interest and personal growth. Since you have 1 week to write a maximum of 1000 words, you only have to write 140-ish words of your own every day, which is a very small amount.
  6. I can tell if it is AI and you will be hit with an APFSDS sabot if found using AI.
  7. Your CSV must mention the starting date of your topic's writing commencement.
  8. Keep the topics interesting, they could be anything: space, games, music interest, etc. (rules are rules ;) ).
  9. By the end of 1 month you will have 4 essays entirely of your own, which will teach you a lot more than just the topics themselves!
